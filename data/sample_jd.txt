Senior Backend Engineer — Cloud Platform

About the role
We are hiring a backend engineer to own the services behind our customer-facing
platform. You will design APIs, run them in production, and work closely with the
platform team on our Kubernetes migration.

Required
- 3+ years of professional experience building backend services in Python
- Strong experience with REST API design (FastAPI, Flask or Django REST)
- Hands-on production experience with AWS (EC2, S3, IAM, EKS or ECS)
- Working knowledge of relational databases, particularly PostgreSQL
- Experience with containerisation using Docker
- Must have experience operating services in production, not only building them

Preferred
- Kubernetes experience in a production environment
- CI/CD pipeline ownership (GitHub Actions, Jenkins or GitLab CI)
- Exposure to infrastructure-as-code (Terraform or CloudFormation)
- Nice to have: experience with event-driven architectures (Kafka, SQS)

Responsibilities
- Design, build and maintain high-throughput REST services
- Improve reliability and reduce latency of existing endpoints
- Participate in on-call rotation and incident response
- Mentor junior engineers through code review

Education
- Bachelor's degree in Computer Science or equivalent practical experience
